1. Introduction

Welcome to the Web-based Order Management System (OMS). This system is designed specifically for ABC Power Equipment Inc. to streamline and automate order processing, bill of materials (BOM) generation, purchase order creation, invoicing, and payment tracking.

The main objective of the OMS is to reduce order processing time, improve invoice accuracy, and enhance the overall on-time delivery rate. This centralized application provides a cohesive and secure environment for all departments to collaborate efficiently.

2. Getting Started

2.1 Accessing the System

The OMS is a web-based application accessible via any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Apple Safari). Navigate to the provided company URL to access the login portal.

2.2 Login Process

  1. Open your web browser and enter the OMS URL.
  2. On the login page, select your Role from the dropdown menu.
  3. Input your registered Email and Password.
  4. Click on the Submit button to login.
  5. If you have forgotten your password, click the "Forgot Password?" link to initiate the reset process via email.

Security Note

Your access is governed by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). The dashboard and modules visible to you will depend entirely on the role assigned to you by the System Administrator.

3. User Roles & Personas

The system caters to different user groups, each with specific permissions:

  • Sales Representative & Customers: Focused on creating, tracking, and managing sales orders.
  • Production Manager & Engineers: Responsible for generating and managing product configurations and detailed Bill of Materials (BOM).
  • Purchasing Officer & Suppliers: Handles the creation and tracking of purchase orders for necessary inventory components.
  • Finance Manager : Manages the generation of invoices, tracking of payments, and reconciliation.
  • Business Analyst & Managers: Accesses high-level reports, trends, and operational dashboards.
  • Administrator : Configures the system, manages users, roles, permissions, and ensures overall data security.

4. Order Management

Primary Users: Sales Representatives, Customers

The Order Management module allows for the efficient creation and tracking of customer sales orders.

4.1 Creating a New Sales Order

  1. Navigate to the Sales Orders tab on the left-hand navigation menu.
  2. Click the + Create Order button at the top right of the screen.
  3. Select the Customer from the dropdown menu (or add a new customer if authorized).
  4. Add the requested Products, specifying the quantity for each. The unit price will automatically populate.
  5. Review the Total Amount and click Submit Order.

4.2 Managing Order Status

  • To update an order, locate it in the Sales Order list and click View/Edit.
  • You can change the status (e.g., Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered) using the Update Status dropdown.
  • Changes automatically trigger notifications to the customer and update the system dashboards.

5. BOM Management

Primary Users: Production Managers, Engineers

This module allows the manufacturing team to generate detailed Bill of Materials based on product configurations.

5.1 Managing Product Configurations

  1. Go to the Products & BOM section.
  2. Select a specific Product to view its details.
  3. Click on the Bill of Materials tab to view the components required to manufacture the product.

5.2 Generating a BOM

  • To link components to a product, click Add Component.
  • Select the component item from the inventory list and specify the required Quantity.
  • Save the configuration. The system utilizes this BOM to deduct inventory appropriately when a sales order is processed or to trigger purchase alerts.

6. Purchase Order Management

Primary Users: Purchasing Officers, Suppliers

Ensure that manufacturing components are always in stock by creating and tracking Purchase Orders (POs) with suppliers.

6.1 Creating a Purchase Order

  1. Navigate to Purchase Orders.
  2. Click + New PO.
  3. Select a Supplier.
  4. Add the required Components/Products and specify quantities.
  5. Verify the total amount and click Generate PO. The PO will be sent directly to the supplier's registered email.

6.2 Tracking PO Status

You can track the status of POs (e.g., Requested, Approved, In Transit, Received). Upon receiving the items physically, update the PO status to Received to automatically update the system's stock inventory levels.

7. Invoice & Payment Management

Primary Users: Finance Managers

Generate invoices seamlessly from completed sales orders and track the incoming payments.

7.1 Generating Invoices

  1. Go to the Invoices tab.
  2. Click Generate Invoice and select a fulfilled Sales Order.
  3. The system will auto-fill customer details, items, and total amounts.
  4. Click Create & Send. This changes the order status to "Invoiced".

7.2 Recording Payments

  1. Navigate to the Payments section.
  2. Click Record Payment.
  3. Select the corresponding Invoice ID.
  4. Enter the Payment Amount, Date, and Payment Method (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
  5. If the payment matches the total invoice amount, the invoice status will automatically update to Paid.

8. Reporting & Analytics

Primary Users: Business Analysts, Managers

Access high-level overviews of company performance, inventory status, and financial health.

8.1 Using the Dashboard

  • The Analytics Dashboard provides real-time graphs and KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) such as Monthly Revenue, Orders Processed, and On-Time Delivery Rate.
  • Use the date-range filter at the top right to analyze specific periods.

8.2 Generating Reports

  1. Go to the Reports tab.
  2. Select the type of report (e.g., Sales Report, Inventory Levels, Financial Summary).
  3. Select the desired date range and click Generate.
  4. Reports can be exported in PDF or Excel (CSV) formats for offline use.

9. User Management & Security

Primary Users: Administrators

Maintain the integrity and security of the system by managing user access.

9.1 Managing Users

  1. Navigate to the Users tab in the navigation menu.
  2. To add a new employee, click the + Add User button.
  3. Enter the user's Name and Email.
  4. Select an appropriate Role from the dropdown (e.g., Finance Manager, Sales Representative).
  5. Click Create User to save.

9.2 Security and Access Controls

Administrators have the capability to suspend accounts, force password resets, and view audit logs to monitor system activity. The system uses strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure that users can only access modules relevant to their job functions.

10. Support & Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues while using the Order Management System:

  • Clear Browser Cache: Often, display or loading issues can be resolved by clearing your browser's cache.
  • Check Internet Connection: Ensure you are connected to a stable network.
  • Contact IT Support: For persistent bugs, password resets (if email reset fails), or role permission issues, please contact the internal IT Support Desk at it-support@abcpower.com or call ext. 1234.
